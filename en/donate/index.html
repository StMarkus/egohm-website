Support

Support eGohm with a donation

eGohm is free, ad-free and collects no data. What the app does cost is devices for testing and tools for development – and that is exactly, and only, what donations pay for.

What donations are used for

Donations are used exclusively for new hardware and AI subscriptions. Both go straight into developing eGohm further.

  • Hardware Test and development devices for Android, iPhone, iPad and Mac – layout, fonts and performance can only be checked reliably on real devices.
  • AI subscriptions Tools used to capture texts, generate transliterations, check data and build new features.
  • Not for salaries Work on eGohm is and remains voluntary. No fees are paid out.

Donate with PayPal

One-off or recurring – you choose the amount in PayPal.

The payment happens entirely at PayPal. This website receives no payment data.

Frequently asked

Is eGohm limited without a donation?

No. All content and features are and remain completely free – no account, no ads, no subscription.

Who is behind eGohm?

The app is published by St. Mark Coptic Orthodox Church Frankfurt e.V. for the Coptic Orthodox Diocese of Southern Germany.
